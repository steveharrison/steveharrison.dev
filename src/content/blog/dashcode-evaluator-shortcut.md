<p><em>Originally published on my old QuintusQuill Console blog in January 2009.</em></p>
<p>I just noticed that Dashcode's evaluator has a Terminal-like shortcut. By pressing the up-arrow key, you can insert a previous statement that you entered.
<p>You can choose which statement you want by pressing the up/down-arrow keys to go back/forward (respectively). When you want to repeatedly execute the same command, this saves a lot of typing!</p></p>
